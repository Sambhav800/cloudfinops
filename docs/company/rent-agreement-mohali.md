# LEAVE AND LICENSE DEED

THIS LICENSE DEED is hereby made at Mohali on this _____ day of July, 2026:

**BETWEEN**

**Mr. Ajay Pal**, residing at Tower-3, SBP Southcity, VIP Road, Zirakpur, Mohali, Punjab (hereinafter called the First Party i.e. LICENSOR)

**AND**

**M/s CloudFinOps Solutions Private Limited**, through its Authorised Signatory **Mr. Sambhav Pal**, Director, R/o Tower-3, SBP Southcity, VIP Road, Zirakpur, Mohali, Punjab (hereinafter called the Second Party i.e. LICENSEE)

---

WHEREAS, the First Party is the lawful owner/occupant of the premises situated at:

**Tower-3, SBP Southcity, VIP Road, Zirakpur, Mohali, Punjab**

And whereas the Second Party is in need of office space for running its business related to Information Technology and Cloud Computing services, as such the Second Party approached the First Party with a request to use and occupy a portion of the above-referred property for use as a **Registered Office / Business Office** for a period of **11 (Eleven) months** on certain terms and conditions which the parties have agreed hereinafter mentioned as below:

---

## NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS UNDER:

1. That the Second Party shall pay to the First Party for the said premises the License fee of **Rs. 1,000/- (Rupees One Thousand Only)** per month payable in advance on or before the 7th of each English calendar month.

2. That the Second Party has paid a sum of **Rs. 1,000/- (Rupees One Thousand Only)** as the first month's License fee.

3. That the Second Party has deposited a sum of **Rs. 1,000/- (Rupees One Thousand Only)** as security deposit which will be refunded only after vacation of the premises and subject to deduction of dues or any breakage, if any.

4. That the License Fee shall be increased at 5% after every one year.

5. That the time period of this agreement is of **Eleven (11) months**, renewable by mutual consent of both parties.

6. That this License Deed starts with effect from **1st August, 2026** for a period of 11 months.

7. That the Licensee shall under no circumstances sublet, assign, transfer or otherwise part with the possession of a part or whole of the premises.

8. That the Licensee shall comply with all the rules and regulations of the local authorities whatsoever with relation to the demised premises.

9. That the Licensee shall not carry out any structural additions or alterations to the building, layout, fitting or fixtures, without the consent of the Licensor.

10. That the Licensee shall not cause or permit to be caused any damage to the demised premises and shall keep the premises harmless.

11. That the Licensee shall be authorized to display and hang sign boards and name plates at all convenient and authorized places in and around the licensed premises.

12. That if any illegal act/activity is done in the licensed premises by the Licensee, it will be the sole responsibility of the Licensee.

13. That on expiry of the License Deed the Licensee shall hand over vacant peaceful possession of the premises to the Licensor. Before leaving the premises, the Licensee should settle all dues, in respect of rent, cost of breakages, if any, due from the Licensee.

14. That if the Licensee shall fail to pay one month's rent regularly or break any term of this agreement, then this agreement shall stand terminated automatically.

15. That the electricity charges shall be paid by the Second Party as per meter installed on monthly basis.

16. That the Licensee agrees not to store or exhibit for sale any combustible and inflammable articles and substances prohibited by insurance company.

17. That in case of withdrawal of the premises, one month advance notice to be given from either side to this effect.

18. That the First Party has no objection to the Second Party (M/s CloudFinOps Solutions Private Limited) using the above-mentioned premises as its **Registered Office / Correspondence Address** for the purpose of registration with the Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Corporate Affairs, and for receiving all statutory communications, notices, and correspondence.

---

In witnesses whereof, the parties have signed the agreement on the day, month and year first above written in the presence of witnesses at Mohali.
